## Q01

The model cap is not binding Karnataka law. Deposit size alone does not decide enforceability.

Sections: MTA-11(1).

Sources: [Source 1](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-model-tenancy-act-2021), [Source 2](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7810/1/34_of_2001_e.pdf).

## Q02

Section 2(3)(f) excludes this recent building. Market rent alone does not establish section 2(3)(e).

Sections: KRA-2(3)(e), KRA-2(3)(f).

Sources: [Source 1](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7810/1/34_of_2001_e.pdf).

## Q03

Part B's threshold is INR 2,000; confirmed statutory rent of INR 3,000 exceeds it.

Sections: KRA-2(3)(e), KRA-SCHEDULE-1-B.

Sources: [Source 1](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7810/1/34_of_2001_e.pdf).

## Q05

No universal ten-percent cap is established by our sources. Ten percent is Dhara's product-review threshold.

Sections: No legal cap asserted; product threshold only.

Sources: [Source 1](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7810/1/34_of_2001_e.pdf), [Source 2](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-model-tenancy-act-2021).

## Q06

Section 74 requires a reasonable-compensation assessment within the stipulated amount; it does not automatically award the entire deposit.

Sections: ICA-74.

Sources: [Source 1](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2187/2/A187209.pdf), [Source 2](https://www.api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2023/6113/6113_2023_13_1502_61408_Judgement_02-May-2025.pdf).

## Q07

The dated checklist and agreement matter. Section 108(m) excepts reasonable wear; section 108's defaults can yield to contrary contract or local usage.

Sections: TPA-108, TPA-108(m).

Sources: [Source 1](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/14648/1/tpa.pdf).

## Q08

Registration Act section 17(1)(d) and TPA section 107 generally require registration for this term; notarisation does not replace it.

Sections: RA-17, TPA-107.

Sources: [Source 1](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/14646/1/ra1908-16.pdf), [Source 2](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/14648/1/tpa.pdf).

## Q09

Section 49 restricts withdrawal without just and sufficient cause where the Act applies. Assess the stated cause, not merely the demand for payment.

Sections: KRA-49.

Sources: [Source 1](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7810/1/34_of_2001_e.pdf).

## Q10

Consider written demand, legal notice and appropriate civil recovery. Do not assume an MTA forum or universal thirty-day deadline; verify forum limits.

Sections: KRA-2(3)(f), KRA-3(c), MTA-30.

Sources: [Source 1](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7810/1/34_of_2001_e.pdf), [Source 2](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-model-tenancy-act-2021).
