# Legal source scope

Verified against official indexed source text on 18 October 2026. The prototype retrieves a curated local source catalogue; it does not perform live legal research for each document.

## Karnataka screening

The [official Karnataka Rent Act consolidation](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7810/1/34_of_2001_e.pdf) includes Act 07 of 2026. Sections 1, 2 and 49 were checked in the official India Code indexed text. Direct PDF downloads intermittently timed out during implementation; source records disclose that verification method.

The encoded section 2 checks cover scheduled-area confirmation, specified ownership/grant exclusions, the fifteen-year construction/substantial-renovation exclusion, and statutory deemed/standard-rent thresholds. **Current monthly rent is not a substitute for statutory rent.** The user must supply and confirm the relevant facts and additional exemption checks.

Possible outcomes are missing jurisdiction, unsupported jurisdiction, unresolved applicability, an encoded exclusion, or conditional legal review. An exclusion does not establish that no other law applies. Conditional review does not replace an authority's applicability determination.

The initial section-level clause rule flags potential essential-service withdrawal conflicts under section 49(1), only after conditional coverage checks pass. It preserves the statutory qualification concerning just and sufficient cause and sends legal findings for human review. This is one legal rule, not comprehensive lease compliance checking.

## Model legislation

The [official Model Tenancy Act announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1723636&lang=2&reg=48) explains adoption through state/UT legislation or amendments. The prototype does not assume the model itself governs a Karnataka lease or turn its suggested deposit cap into an applicable state-law requirement.

## Preferences and language

Deposit, notice, refund, access and rent-increase checks compare supported English numeric wording with the user's preferences. They are labeled separately from potential legal conflicts. The Kannada/Hindi drafts use complete templates for structured requests; they do not translate arbitrary uploaded clauses or negotiated prose. Accepted original wording is preserved separately. Native-language review has not been performed.

## Boundaries

Other states, RERA, commercial leases, litigation predictions, general registration/stamp-duty analysis, and comprehensive statutory compliance are outside this MVP. Source freshness, factual correctness and unmodeled exemptions require further verification before real-world reliance.
