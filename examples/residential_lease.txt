1. Rent and receipts
Monthly rent is INR 20,000 payable by the fifth day. The landlord provides payment receipts.

2. Security deposit
The security deposit is 6 months of rent and is non-refundable.

3. Termination
The tenant must give 90 days notice to terminate the agreement.

4. Landlord access
The landlord may enter without notice for routine inspections.

5. Repairs
The landlord pays structural repairs; the tenant pays routine maintenance.

6. Property
This synthetic agreement concerns a residential apartment in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
